---
name: sage-explain
description: Explain a technical or scientific mechanism from first principles when the user asks for conceptual understanding.
---

Identify the user's specific uncertainty and the level of detail that resolves
it. Establish the relevant quantities, units, assumptions, and observable behavior.
Build from a small concrete example to the mechanism and its consequences. For
a discrepancy, trace the inputs through actual transformations and denominators.
Separate established facts, evidence-based inference, and hypotheses.
Use an analogy only when it clarifies the mechanism, and state its material limit.
Use a diagram or interactive example when relationships or changing parameters
are easier to inspect visually. Use standard exported plots for scientific work.
Retrieve primary documentation for niche or version-sensitive facts. Keep the
answer focused on the user's question rather than introducing a general tutorial.
