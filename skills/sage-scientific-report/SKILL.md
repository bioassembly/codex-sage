---
name: sage-scientific-report
description: Analyze and visualize scientific results or explain discrepancies in genomic reports.
---

Establish the unit of analysis, cohort, reference, preprocessing, normalization,
missingness, and denominator before comparing numbers. Trace discrepancies from
raw records through filters and aggregation; use a small worked example grounded
in the actual data. Distinguish read, molecule, gene, taxon, and sample counts.
Account for batch effects, repeated measures, confounding, multiple testing, and
compositional data where applicable. Report effect sizes and uncertainty alongside
statistical significance; do not present exploratory associations as causation.
Choose plots that preserve distributions and uncertainty. Label units, transforms,
normalization, sample size, exclusions, and legends. Avoid misleading axis truncation
and verify category order, scales, and color accessibility. Use standard plotting
tools for scientific figures and inspect the exported artifact.
Use the existing Quarto/notebook/report format. Record tool/reference versions,
parameters, data provenance, and exact reproduction commands. Keep machine paths,
private identifiers, and secrets out of shared reports. Label hypotheses and
unsupported interpretations explicitly; a polished figure is not validation.
